# Videos: what to watch

Curated English-language channels and videos that pair well with Cosmic Codex. This file mirrors the *Watch* section of the [Library page](../site/library.html) and the `channels` / `videos` arrays in [`site/data/library.json`](../site/data/library.json).

Every link was checked against web search results on 26 September 2026. Channel names are shown as text only (no logos).

## Channels

### Agency

| Channel | Why watch |
|---|---|
| [NASA](https://www.youtube.com/@NASA) | Launch broadcasts, mission briefings and science explainers straight from the agency. |
| [NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)](https://www.youtube.com/@NASAJPL) | Mars landings, deep-space missions and the 'what's up' night-sky series. |
| [NASA Goddard](https://www.youtube.com/@NASAGoddard) | Stunning data visualisations of the Sun, Earth, Moon and Webb from the Scientific Visualization Studio. |
| [European Space Agency (ESA)](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCIBaDdAbGlFDeS33shmlD0A) | Missions, astronauts and Earth observation from Europe's space agency. |

## Suggested viewing order

1. **Rockets:** Primal Space on Saturn V staging, then Smarter Every Day on steering it, then Everyday Astronaut on engine cycles.
2. **Landing:** Scott Manley's hoverslam, then JPL's *Seven Minutes of Terror*.
3. **Spacecraft:** Veritasium on rotating bodies (attitude control), then JPL on the Deep Space Network.
4. **The bigger picture:** ESA on space debris, Kurzgesagt on Mars bases, and Apollo 11 in Real Time for an evening you will not forget.
