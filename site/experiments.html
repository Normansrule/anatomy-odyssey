Safety is part of the build

Model rockets fly only on certified, commercially made motors under the National Association of Rocketry (NAR) Model Rocket Safety Code, Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) rules in 14 CFR Part 101 and the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) 1122. Nothing here teaches making propellant, motors, igniters or anything pyrotechnic. Water rockets stay at or below 60 psi with eye protection and a remote pull-string release. Never look at the Sun. Under 18? Build with an adult.
