    One sky, many skies

    Every survey on the rail looks at the same sphere of sky. What changes is the wavelength of light, and each wavelength is made by different physics. Change the band and different things light up.

    Temperature picks the colour

    Anything warm glows, and the hotter it is the shorter the wavelength where it glows brightest. Wien's displacement law gives the peak wavelength for a temperature T:

    λpeak = b / T,   b = 2.898 × 10−3 m·K

    • 2.7 K Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) → about 1 mm, microwave
    • 20 K cold dust → 145 µm, far-infrared
    • 300 K warm dust (and you) → 10 µm, mid-infrared
    • 5,800 K the Sun's surface → 500 nm, visible
    • 30,000 K hot young stars → 97 nm, ultraviolet
    • 10 million K cluster gas → 0.3 nm, X-ray

    Dust hides, then glows

    Grains of interstellar dust are about the size of the wavelength of blue light, so they scatter and absorb visible light very well and long infrared light hardly at all. Toward the Galactic Centre dust dims visible light by about 30 magnitudes, a factor of 1012; in the near-infrared the loss is only about 2.5 magnitudes. The dust itself, warmed by starlight, re-emits that energy in the far-infrared.

    Gas speaks in lines and sparks

    Some light is not thermal at all. Hydrogen re-forming after being ionised emits the red hydrogen-alpha line at 656.3 nm. Electrons spiralling through magnetic fields at nearly light speed emit synchrotron radio waves. Cosmic-ray protons hitting gas make gamma rays. Radio and gamma maps are photographs of particles and magnetism, not of heat.

    Why most of these came from space

    Earth's atmosphere only lets through visible light, some near-infrared and radio. Water vapour absorbs most infrared and millimetre waves, and ozone and the air above it stop ultraviolet (UV) light, X-rays and gamma rays, so Planck, AKARI, WISE, GALEX, eROSITA and Fermi had to fly.     How the tiles work

    Each survey is cut into a Hierarchical Progressive Survey (HiPS): the sphere is divided with the Hierarchical Equal Area isoLatitude Pixelization (HEALPix) into 12 base tiles, and each level splits every tile into four. Level k has 12 × 4k tiles of 512 × 512 pixels, so only the tiles in view, at the detail your screen needs, are downloaded.
