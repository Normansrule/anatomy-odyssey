Credits and licences

NASA photographs are generally not copyrighted; they are credited here as NASA asks. Hubble and Webb images from the ESA/Hubble and ESA/Webb portals, and Event Horizon Telescope (EHT) images published by the European Southern Observatory (ESO), are licensed Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). Every image links back to its source page. Use of NASA material does not imply endorsement.

Offline copy

Images load live from NASA, ESA, ESO and Wikimedia servers. If one fails, the page searches the NASA Image and Video Library for a stand-in, and otherwise shows a titled placeholder. To keep a local copy of every photograph, run this once from the repository root:

python3 scripts/fetch_gallery.py

It saves the images to site/assets/gallery/ and writes site/data/gallery.local.json, which this page prefers when present.
