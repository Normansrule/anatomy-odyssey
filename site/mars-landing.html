Why landing on Mars is so hard

Mars' atmosphere is too thick to ignore and too thin to rely on. Thick enough that a spacecraft arriving at 5.3 km/s needs a heat shield; so thin that a parachute alone still leaves you falling at about 80 m/s near the ground. And the planet is 11 light-minutes away, so nobody on Earth can help.
