NASA project life cycle

From idea to closeout

Every NASA flight project follows the same life cycle, set out in NASA Procedural Requirements (NPR) 7120.5 and explained in the NASA Systems Engineering Handbook (SP-2016-6105 Rev 2, Figure 3.0-1). Formulation (Pre-Phase A to B) decides what to build; Implementation (C to F) builds, flies and retires it. Between phases sit Key Decision Points (KDPs), where the agency decides whether to continue.

    This page is a Pre-Phase A study. Its output — a concept that closes, with margins — is what a Mission Concept Review (MCR) examines before Key Decision Point A.
